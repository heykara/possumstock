PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 21, 2026

Your privacy matters: PossumStock uses website information only to operate, secure, and improve the festival site and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Our hosting and service providers may receive standard technical information when you use the website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, referring page, and timestamps.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website.
  • Understand site performance and how visitors use the website.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized activity.
  • Support festival operations and respond to inquiries.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

We may provide information to service providers that help host, secure, analyze, or maintain the website and support event operations. These providers may use the information only to provide services to PossumStock or as required by law.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal process, protect the rights or safety of PossumStock or others, investigate abuse, or complete a business transfer involving the festival or its operations.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the website, support event operations, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or de-identified.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

The PossumStock site includes links or content from third parties, such as mapping, ticketing, social media, font, and hosting providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

This site also links to the PossumStock 2026 On-Site Guide, a separate web app you can add to your home screen. It is a distinct application on its own address, and anything it stores on your device or handles when you enable features such as set reminders is governed by that app rather than by this policy. Following the link from this site does not send us any personal information.

7. Children’s Privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and PossumStock does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the website, event, or related services change. The revised policy becomes effective when posted with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact PossumStock through @POSSUMSTOCKTN on Instagram.
